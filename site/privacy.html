Privacy

The short version: this site counts its readers with software we run ourselves. Nothing about you is sold, shared, or sent to an ad network, because none of those things exist here.

What we measure

musterr.dev uses Matomo, an open-source analytics tool, self-hosted on our own server. For each visit it records:

  • the pages you view and the site that referred you here,
  • your browser and device type,
  • your approximate location (city level), derived from an anonymized IP address. The last two octets are discarded before anything is stored, so 203.0.113.7 is kept only as 203.0.0.0. We cannot identify you from it.

Cookies

None. Matomo runs here in cookieless mode: it sets no _pk_id or session cookie and stores nothing in your browser. The trade-off is ours, not yours. A returning visitor is counted as a new one, because there is nothing on your device to recognize. There is no third-party ad tech, no fingerprinting service, and no social widget on this site.

Interaction heatmaps

To see which parts of a page actually get used, the site also sends small anonymous interaction beacons (where on the page clicks land and how far down you scroll, both as percentages) to analogelk.com, infrastructure we run ourselves. A beacon carries only those coordinates, the page path, the size of your browser window, and a coarse device class (mobile or desktop). It records no text and no identifiers, sets no cookies, and cannot be tied to you.

Privacy signals

  • Global Privacy Control (Sec-GPC): if your browser sends it, neither the analytics script nor the interaction beacon runs. No analytics request of any kind leaves your browser.
  • Do Not Track: the analytics server discards visits that carry it. The interaction beacon does not check this older signal, so the anonymous click and scroll percentages above are still sent. Use Global Privacy Control if you want both off.

Fonts

Pages load their typefaces from Google Fonts. Like any web request, that tells Google your IP address and that a page here was opened. It sets no cookies. Global Privacy Control does not change it.

Retention

  • Raw visit logs are deleted after 13 months; only aggregate statistics (counts, not visits) are kept beyond that.
  • All analytics and beacon data stays on infrastructure we operate. No third party receives it.

The demo portal

The demo at app.musterr.dev is a static snapshot of the portal. There is no login, no account, and no session token. The page keeps a small data cache in your browser's local storage so views load faster. It loads nothing from third parties. The portal software still tries to send page-view, performance, and click events back to app.musterr.dev, but the snapshot is plain files with no server behind them, so those requests are answered with a redirect and nothing is recorded.
