Photo: private albums for clients.

A short case study. Photo is internal only and is not in the Muster release.

Case study

Photo is not in the Muster release.

Photo is an internal tool we built on the same records as the rest of the back office. It is not part of Muster. There is nothing to install here.

Internal only, not in the release. This page shows no screenshots and no real albums, clients, or addresses.

What it does.

  • Private albums. Each album is private.
  • Magic links. A client opens an album from a magic link.
  • Favorites. A client marks the photos they want.
  • Zip downloads. A client downloads an album as one zip file.
  • Tips. A client can leave a tip.

Why it is on this site.

It shows what a studio can build on a shared set of records. Albums, clients, and tasks live in one place. Agents and people read the same rows.
